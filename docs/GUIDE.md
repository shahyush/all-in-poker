# All In — host, player and install guide

## Players
1. Open the link the host sent you (or the app URL, https://all-in-poker.pages.dev/) on your phone. Enter a nickname and the **group password**. That is the
   whole sign-up; the app creates a private player for this device.
2. **Save your player code** (Profile → *Save my player code*) once. It restores *your* player on another phone or after
   clearing the browser. Nobody else can use it to become you without it, and it is never in an invite link.
3. Join a table with the 8-character invite code or the invite link. Pick your buy-in (a multiple of the table's unit) and a
   seat. Joining is the only moment chips are added to your stack.
4. Your turn: the action bar shows only what is legal — Fold, Check or Call *(amount)*, Bet/Raise to *(amount)* with
   min/max and the pot cap. Amounts are whole chips. The timer is the server's; running out checks if possible, otherwise folds.
   Two timeouts in a row sit you out at a cash table.
5. Dealer's choice: when the button reaches you, you have 4 seconds to pick the game; otherwise the table default deals.
6. Run it twice: on the flop or turn, before anyone bets, the player to act may propose it. Everyone still in has 8 seconds to
   agree; your own clock resumes where it left off. When everybody is all in, the table offers once/twice for 5 seconds.
7. Taking a break: *Sit out* takes effect after the current hand; *Return* puts you back (post to play now, or wait for the
   blind). *Leave* cashes out after the hand; your history stays in **History**.
8. Reloads: ask for an amount; the host approves; chips arrive between hands. The session summary shows added, taken off,
   and current stack for everyone, and the ledger always balances.

## Hosts
1. **Create a table**: cash or tournament, name, blinds, ante, straddle on/off, unit, seats (2–9; Omaha-6 needs ≤7 players
   because nothing is burned), limits per game. Defaults are proposals; everything can change until *Start*.
2. **Invite**: the waiting room shows the code and a share button. Invites are private to the group password.
3. **Start** when at least two funded players are seated. Settings lock at start.
4. During play you approve reloads, remove a player (they fold out and cash out after the hand), or transfer host. If you
   vanish for 30 seconds the longest-seated present player becomes host automatically; nothing stalls.
5. **Tournaments**: set the level schedule (minutes, blinds, ante). Levels change on the clock; busts are ranked; the last
   player wins; a deserted event is marked abandoned after 30 minutes of nobody present.
6. **Rotate the group password** (Profile → owner section) whenever you want to close the door to old links.

## Install on iPhone (Safari)
1. Open the app URL in Safari. 2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**. 3. Launch from the icon: it opens full
screen, remembers your player, and resumes your seat. Updates arrive on the next launch; the app never reloads mid-hand.

## Install on Android (Chrome)
Open the app URL → menu ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** (or the install banner).

## Rules in one breath
No burn cards · dealer's choice from hold'em, Omaha, Omaha-5 and Omaha-6 · moving button in cash games, dead button in
tournaments · missed blinds are owed (post to play or wait for the big blind) · pot-limit caps use the pot after your call ·
a short all-in raise does not reopen betting · odd chips go clockwise from the button, board A takes the odd chip when run
twice · uncontested winners get 3 seconds to show · the next hand starts 3 seconds after awards.
